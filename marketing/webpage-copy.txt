Open Road Ramble
Take the long way: a cozy 3D drive, rare cars and friendly fuel stops.

Settle into a third-person drive through meadows, coast, desert and alpine scenery. Your little hatchback has a full road ahead: passing travelers, rare vehicles to spot and a warm fuel-and-coffee stop around the next bend.

THREE SELLING CARDS
A road worth wandering
A following 3D camera, four landscapes and a distance-based day/night cycle make room for the next small discovery.

A proper little pit stop
Pull beneath the fuel canopy, fill the tank for free and continue. Friendly help is there when the tank or your three hearts run out.

A travel notebook that grows
Collect cars and rare traffic discoveries, buy upgrades and follow daily detours at your own pace.
