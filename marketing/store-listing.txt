Open Road Ramble

SHORT DESCRIPTION (70/80 characters)
Take the long way: a cozy 3D drive, rare cars and friendly fuel stops.

LONG DESCRIPTION
A little road. A big sky. Take the long way.

Settle into a third-person drive through meadows, coast, desert and alpine scenery. Your little hatchback has a full road ahead: passing travelers, rare vehicles to spot and a warm fuel-and-coffee stop around the next bend.

Drag to steer and hold the brake when you want a slower view. Traffic bumps have consequences: three hearts, a loss of speed and a reason to leave a little room. Friendly roadside help and free fuel keep a mistake from ending the journey. Your banked progress stays with you.

Tour by day and follow your headlights after dark. Discover unusual cars for your roadside journal, collect five cars, improve your tank and comfort, and grow your rambler level over many small trips. Daily detours offer goals for distance, clean passes and fuel stops when you want a little direction.

The core journey works offline and saves locally. Android includes optional rewarded bonuses and ads at between-trip transitions. Keep the tank topped up, wave to the next traveler and see what is beyond the bend.
